About The Stream by Ekantah

A hidden 5-room boutique stay by the river in Tirthan Valley — The Stream by Ekantah offers calm, charm, and connection to nature.

Enjoy exclusive access to a private stream, unwind at our riverside café, and spend your evenings with good music, bonfires, movie nights, and karaoke in our cozy common room. We also have games, books, and plenty of corners to just pause.

If you're looking to slow down, breathe deep, and stay somewhere that feels like a story — this is the place.

The space
Our boutique riverside retreat features five charming rooms, each designed to offer a peaceful and homely stay in the lap of nature.

Three of our rooms face the river and come with private balconies, letting you unwind to the sights and sounds of flowing water.

Every room has a private attached washroom and is thoughtfully furnished with warm, cozy interiors that feel just like home.

The rooms are consistent in layout and comfort, blending simplicity with soft bohemian touches.

Guest access

Exclusive stream access right from the property—ideal for morning dips or quiet reflection

A short and scenic river trek leading to a hidden waterfall just 50 meters away

A charming bonfire area under the stars for shared stories and warmth
